COMMON QUESTIONS

Know exactly what you're evaluating.

Does Zynvero run locally?

The client CRM and AI workflows are designed for local deployment, with a vendor administration and licensing component.

Which AI model does it use?

The demonstrated local implementation used Ollama with qwen2.5:3b for qualification testing. Model selection and performance depend on local hardware and configuration.

Can it qualify leads in different languages?

Local qualification tests covered multilingual inputs, including Hindi and Arabic. Real-world results depend on the selected model and use case.

Are WhatsApp and social channels already live?

Local demos include simulated inbound flows for WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, website and Google. Production connections require real provider configuration and are not claimed to be live by this website.

What does the sales team see?

Lead records, conversation histories, statuses, assignment, notes, qualification context and role-based views are part of the demonstrated CRM workflow.

How does licensing work?

The local CRM includes vendor-side signed-license issuance and verification flows. Subscription and access rules can be administered separately from the local client installation.
